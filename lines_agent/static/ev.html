How the fair price and EV are calculated
  1. Each sharp price becomes an implied probability: p = 1 / decimal odds.
  2. The vig is removed three ways, each sharp book on its own:
    • Multiplicative: fair = pside / (pside + pother). Every side gives up the same share of its probability, so the favorite absorbs most of the vig.
    • Shin: models the book guarding against a share z of insiders and solves for z. It takes more vig off the longshot than multiplicative does.
    • Power: fair = pk, with k solved so the two sides sum to 1. It takes the most vig off the longshot.
    Near even money the three agree. On big underdogs they can differ by 20+ points of EV, so a longshot that clears your minimum under every method (All three must clear) is the most trustworthy. The sharp books are then blended into a consensus by a weighted average of the books posting that exact market and number.
  3. EV at your book: EV = fair × decimal odds − 1. Kelly fraction: EV / (decimal − 1); the stake shown is ¼ Kelly of your bankroll.

Spreads and totals are only compared at the same number as the sharp line. Pre-game only. An edge of 20% or more on a main line is tagged CHECK: it usually means a stale or mismatched price, so confirm it at the book before betting.
